Depot Level

Direct action-takers. They receive instant notifications for feedback assigned to their specific depot via trip matching.

Division Level

Monitors performance across multiple depots. They ensure service standards are met and bottlenecks are identified.

Central Level

High-level strategic oversight. Accesses state-wide reporting and trends to drive policy and quality improvements.
